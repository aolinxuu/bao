questions, answered.

is it free?

yes. texting Peku is free. you pay the restaurant, same as always.

which cities?

San Francisco for now. outside SF, Peku will tell you honestly it doesn't know the area well enough.

what do you do with my data?

Peku uses what you text it to find and book spots and to learn your taste. the full details are in the privacy policy.

can it book anywhere?

anywhere that takes reservations online. if a spot doesn't, Peku says so and sends you the direct link.
